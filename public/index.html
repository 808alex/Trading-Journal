FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Locally, in a SQLite file on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

How do I close a trade I logged earlier?

Trade List → Open Positions → click the trade → fill in the exit details → Close Trade.

How do I see P&L in a different currency?

Set a default currency and rate in Settings, then use the SOL/{currency} switch on Total P&L or the Calendar.

What do the trade grades mean?

They grade your process, not your profit — A means you followed your thesis and plan regardless of outcome, D means a fully random or FOMO entry. The full breakdown shows up whenever you're grading a trade.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

Not automatically — it's a local file (in this project's data/ folder) you'd need to back up yourself.
